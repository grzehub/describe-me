The stage's canvas takes the background of the page in its frame. When a frame's `html`, or its `body` when `html` paints none, has a `background-color` at least half opaque, the canvas around the frame gets that color under `auto`, `light` and `dark`, in replay, in Live and in the overview's thumbnails. A page that paints its body shows no band at its edges. `transparent` keeps the checkerboard. `auto` takes the color scheme the page declares on its root, or dark when it paints `html` or `body` dark, as before, and otherwise follows the viewer theme: the theme picked in the header, or the system's scheme under `system`. A frame that `auto` puts on the dark canvas gets the dark color scheme and the dark variant, as under `bg=dark`. Under `auto` a change of the viewer theme repaints the stage, the thumbnails and Live, without reloading the live frame. Under `light`, `dark` and `transparent` the viewer theme changes nothing, and Foundations ignores it as before. A replayed frame on the dark canvas whose CSS has no dark variant gets a note that it has only a light variant, and with Material UI that `cssVariables: true` records both. The live page reports the computed `background-color` of `<html>` and `<body>` just before its status and after every change. `LiveReport` in `@describe-me/core/types` has a `background` member with `html` and `body` strings, so code that handles every report type gets one more. A viewer of an earlier version ignores the report, and with a live page that never sends it the canvas keeps the switch's color, so `LIVE_PROTOCOL` stays 1.
