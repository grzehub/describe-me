The viewer has a Foundations view for the design tokens in `foundations.json`. When the run found tokens, a Foundations entry at the top of the sidebar names the design systems and opens one section per group, each with its count: colors, typography, spacing, radius, shadows, breakpoints, motion and other. Specimens render in a sandboxed iframe with the project's own token and font stylesheets, so fonts and `var()` are real. A value that refers to other tokens shows what it resolves to, such as `var(--radius) → 0.625rem`. A theme module wins over Material UI's `--mui-*` variables, the Tailwind theme wins over the variables Tailwind writes, and `@theme inline` color aliases give way to the custom properties they point at. The stage background switch sits at the top of the view. `dark` shows the dark variant that the project declares with a `.dark` class, a `[data-*="dark"]` attribute, `prefers-color-scheme`, `light-dark()` or the dark mode of a theme module. The inspector names the systems, the Tailwind entry, the theme module, the stylesheets, the dark variant and the problems. The link keeps the section as `foundations=<section>`. The viewer fetches `foundations.json` only when the view opens.
