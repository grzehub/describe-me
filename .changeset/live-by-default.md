Live is the default. When the live preview is ready, the selected test opens in Live instead of its recorded frame. `live=0` in the link opens the recorded frame 0 and `frame` opens that recorded frame, so links to frames keep working. A `live=1` link of an earlier version still opens Live. In Live the link has neither `live` nor `frame`. `L` and the Live toggle switch, ← → and a click on the timeline leave Live, and ↑ ↓ and the sidebar keep the mode. Without a live preview the viewer shows the recorded frames as before. While the preview starts the recorded frame shows, and the test switches to Live once it is ready.
