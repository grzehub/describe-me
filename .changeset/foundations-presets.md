Foundations shows the structure of Tailwind 4, shadcn/ui and Material UI. Tailwind gets its project color ramps as large swatches, its default palette in a compact grid with a note on how to remove it, the type scale with line heights, font families, weights, tracking and leading, a spacing scale of `--spacing` multiples, box, inset, drop and text shadows, breakpoints and containers, easing, animations and transitions. shadcn/ui gets its background and foreground pairs as an `Aa` specimen with the WCAG contrast ratio and level for the mode on screen, its chart and sidebar colors, and `--radius` with the `--radius-*` derived from it. Material UI gets its palette by intent with `main` behind `contrastText`, the greys as a ramp, text, background and divider, the 13 typography variants, `spacing(n)`, shape, breakpoints, the 25 elevations, z-index and transitions, from a theme module or from the `--mui-*` variables of `cssVariables: true`. Without either, the view says what to set. Other design systems get blocks by name prefix, such as `--chakra-colors-*`. Framework defaults carry a `default` badge, and blocks have readable names. A filter matches tokens by name and value, and `/` focuses it while Foundations is open.
